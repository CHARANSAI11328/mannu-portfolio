# Administrator CMS Guide: Shaik Mansoor Personal Fitness Platform

Welcome, Shaik Mansoor! This portal enables you to manage your personal training website, programs, monthly pricing, achievements, and WhatsApp contact settings without touching any source code.

---

## 1. Accessing the Admin Portal

- **URL**: Navigate to `/admin/login` on your website domain.
- **Initial Credentials**: Use your provisioned super administrator account.
- **Session Security**: If you step away from your device, remember to click **Log Out** at the bottom of the sidebar.

---

## 2. Managing the Hero Video & Visual Art Direction

Navigate to **Admin > Website Editor & Hero** (`/admin/editor`):
1. **Desktop Background Video**:
   - Paste or upload loopable horizontal footage of yourself training in the gym (1920x1080 recommended).
   - Set focal point to `Center-Right` so your athletic silhouette balances with the headline on the left.
2. **Monochrome Styling & Contrast**:
   - Adjust the **Brightness** slider (80–100%) and **Contrast** slider (110–130%) to achieve the high-contrast aesthetic.
   - Adjust the **Darkness Overlay** slider (70–80%) to ensure text remains readable.
3. **Typography**:
   - Edit the primary headline (default: `TRAIN SMARTER. GET STRONGER.`).
   - Edit the primary and secondary descriptions.
4. **Live Preview**:
   - Click **Show Live Hero Preview** at the top right to verify your changes in real-time before publishing.

---

## 3. Updating Monthly Pricing Plans

Navigate to **Admin > Monthly Plans** (`/admin/pricing`):
- Three initial plans are seeded:
  1. **FOUNDATION** — ₹999/month (Beginners)
  2. **TRANSFORMATION** — ₹1,999/month (Goal-oriented lifters, highlighted)
  3. **ELITE COACHING** — ₹3,999/month (1-on-1 personal mentorship)
- To change a price or add features, click **Edit Pricing & Features**.
- **Important Rule**: Session counts and schedules are flexible and agreed directly between you and your client. Always maintain the session disclaimer.

---

## 4. Managing Training Programs

Navigate to **Admin > Training Programs** (`/admin/programs`):
- You can add, edit, or archive any of the 6 core fitness programs (Muscle Building, Fat Loss, Strength Training, Bodybuilding Preparation, Beginner Fitness, General Conditioning).
- Toggle **Training Modes** between `online` and `offline` depending on what you currently offer.

---

## 5. Adding Competitive Achievements

Navigate to **Admin > Achievements** (`/admin/achievements`):
- Maintain your verified competitive bodybuilding records:
  - 1st Place — Bodybuilding Competition, Bapatla
  - 2nd Place — Bodybuilding Competition, ANU
- Click **+ Add Achievement** when you participate in new competitions.

---

## 6. Client Transformations & Written Consent

Navigate to **Admin > Transformations** (`/admin/transformations`):
- **Client Privacy Protection**: Per strict privacy rules, client progress photos cannot be published without explicit written consent.
- Check the **Client Written Consent Form Verified & On File** checkbox before toggling a transformation to published.
- *Note*: If you do not have any transformations uploaded, the entire section remains hidden on the public site to keep the layout clean.

---

## 7. Contact Details & WhatsApp Number

Navigate to **Admin > Contact & Studio** (`/admin/contact`):
- Enter your WhatsApp number in international format (e.g., `+919876543210`).
- This updates all &quot;GET IN TOUCH&quot;, &quot;SELECT PLAN&quot;, and floating inquiry buttons across the entire website.
- Update studio availability notes, gym address, or Google Maps link as needed.

---

## 8. Draft vs. Publish Workflow

- Whenever you edit content in the CMS, it is saved in your private draft workspace.
- To push all changes live to your public website, click the black button **&quot;Publish Live Changes&quot;** in the top navigation bar.
- Each publication automatically saves a version snapshot. If you ever need to restore an earlier version, visit **Security & Account > Revision History** and click **Restore Snapshot**.
